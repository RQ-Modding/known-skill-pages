# Changelog

Each section is a version of the mod, the `version` in `mod.json`. The release workflow copies the
section of a tagged version into the release notes.

## 1.0.0

First version, from a player's suggestion.
- A character can know up to 34 skills (Config, Mods tab: 17 to 51), and every one of them works
  in fights, on the hotbar and in the stats.
- The status page shows the known skills 17 at a time with a slim scroll bar and the mouse wheel;
  the skill window has two arrows beside KNOWN SKILLS.
- The save stays the game's; the skills past its rows are kept beside it, and a lower setting keeps
  the ones that no longer fit until there is room.
